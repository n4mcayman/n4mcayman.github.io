Electricity fuel-charge cap and fuel duty waiver now run to 31 December

31 Dec 2026

Government extended the 2026 Fuel Relief Programme by three months. The residential electricity fuel charge stays capped at CI$0.18 per kWh, and the 100% import-duty waiver on gasoline, diesel and propane continues.

Radio Cayman (Government): Government extends fuel relief programme through December 2026 · Published 29 Sep 2026

The waiver removes the Government duty; it does not set or freeze pump prices. The release's example saving is about $70.44 a month on 1,200 kWh at September's fuel rate.

Duty-free allowance is CI$800 now and CI$1,000 from 23 November

7 Jan 2027

The standard allowance for returning residents rose from CI$500 to CI$800 on 1 September. A temporary CI$1,000 allowance applies for Christmas travel, 23 November 2026 to 7 January 2027, then CI$800 resumes on 8 January.

GOV.KY: Cabinet Approves Increased Duty Allowances for Returning Residents · Checked 8 Oct 2026

For the summer window the release said the higher allowance applied at the discretion of Customs and Border Control. Check declaration rules with CBC before you travel.

Government extended the 2026 Fuel Relief Programme by three months. The residential electricity fuel charge stays capped at CI$0.18 per kWh, and the 100% import-duty waiver on gasoline, diesel and propane continues.

Starts / deadline
1 Oct 2026
Ends
31 Dec 2026
