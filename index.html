Free shuttle now links East End and North Side

Free

The Eastern Link is a free four-month public transport pilot that started 1 October. It runs Monday to Saturday, 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, not on public holidays. If you transfer to an existing public bus, the normal bus fare applies.

Radio Cayman (Government): The Eastern Link Connecting East End and North Side to Launch 1 October · Published 29 Sep 2026

The release names three services and Frank Sound Junction as the connection to existing buses; stop lists were attachments not shown on the page. No end date is given beyond four months. The four-month duration is stated, but an exact end date is not supplied.

Electricity fuel-charge cap and fuel duty waiver now run to 31 December

31 Dec 2026

Government extended the 2026 Fuel Relief Programme by three months. The residential electricity fuel charge stays capped at CI$0.18 per kWh, and the 100% import-duty waiver on gasoline, diesel and propane continues.

Radio Cayman (Government): Government extends fuel relief programme through December 2026 · Published 29 Sep 2026

The waiver removes the Government duty; it does not set or freeze pump prices. The release's example saving is about $70.44 a month on 1,200 kWh at September's fuel rate.

Duty-free allowance is CI$800 now and CI$1,000 from 23 November

7 Jan 2027

The standard allowance for returning residents rose from CI$500 to CI$800 on 1 September. A temporary CI$1,000 allowance applies for Christmas travel, 23 November 2026 to 7 January 2027, then CI$800 resumes on 8 January.

GOV.KY: Cabinet Approves Increased Duty Allowances for Returning Residents · Checked 10 Oct 2026

For the summer window the release said the higher allowance applied at the discretion of Customs and Border Control. Check declaration rules with CBC before you travel.

Government schools are closed to students from Thursday 15 to Friday 23 October

23 Oct 2026

Two teacher training days on 15 and 16 October run straight into the half-term break of 19 to 23 October. That is seven school days in a row with no classes.

GOV.KY: Cayman Islands Government Schools 2026-2027 School Calendar (approved April 2026) · Published 30 Apr 2026

From the approved calendar PDF. The calendar lists no closure on Monday 26 October. Confirm with your child's school.
