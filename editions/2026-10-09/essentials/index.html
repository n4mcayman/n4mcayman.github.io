Importing a car? Model year eight years or older is prohibited

Check

Customs and Border Control reminded the public on 14 September that vehicles whose model year is eight years or older cannot be imported, subject to listed exemptions. It asks buyers to check before purchasing as the end of the year approaches.

Radio Cayman (Government): CBC Reminds Public of Vehicle Import Restrictions · Published 14 Sep 2026

Exemptions are in Section 4A(2) of the Prohibited Goods Order; the release does not list them. Ask CBC before you buy.

active · All islands

Driving licence fees changed on 1 September: discounts for longer licences, reduced fee at 65Read notice

Caymanians buying three-, five- and ten-year licences get 10%, 15% and 20% off. Customers aged 65 or older qualify for the reduced fee, including non-Caymanian seniors. Non-Caymanian full licence fees are about three times the Caymanian rate, down from the eight times first approved.

Starts / deadline
1 Sep 2026
Ends
Not stated by source

If you think the wrong fee was applied, go to a DVDL office with your receipt and ID. The release gives ratios and discounts, not a full price list.

Eligibility & evidence

DVDL uses Government records to decide which fee applies. If it cannot verify you electronically, the transaction goes to manual verification: bring valid ID and supporting documents.

Documents to prepare

  • Valid identification
  • Supporting status documents if asked
