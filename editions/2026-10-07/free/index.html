Free shuttle now links East End and North Side

Free

The Eastern Link is a free four-month public transport pilot that started 1 October. It runs Monday to Saturday, 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, not on public holidays. If you transfer to an existing public bus, the normal bus fare applies.

Radio Cayman (Government): The Eastern Link Connecting East End and North Side to Launch 1 October · Published 29 Sep 2026

The release names three services and Frank Sound Junction as the connection to existing buses; stop lists were attachments not shown on the page. No end date is given beyond four months. The four-month duration is stated, but an exact end date is not supplied.
